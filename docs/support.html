Frequently Asked Questions

How does PhotoSlim work?

PhotoSlim uses on-device AI powered by Apple's Vision framework to scan your photo library. It detects duplicate photos, blurry images, old screenshots, and groups photos by faces. All processing happens locally on your device.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. PhotoSlim processes everything 100% on your device. Your photos never leave your iPhone or iPad. We do not collect, upload, or share any of your photo data. There are zero third-party analytics or tracking SDKs in the app.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

You can upgrade to PhotoSlim Pro with a one-time purchase of $9.99. Tap the "Upgrade to Pro" button in the app or go to Settings. This is a lifetime purchase with no recurring charges.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

The free version lets you scan your entire library and view all detected duplicates, blurry photos, and screenshots. The Pro version unlocks unlimited photo deletion, face groups, and scan history.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases". This will restore your Pro upgrade if you previously purchased it on the same Apple ID.

Can I recover deleted photos?

Yes. When you delete photos through PhotoSlim, they are moved to the "Recently Deleted" album in your Photos app. You have 30 days to recover them before they are permanently deleted.

Why does the scan take a long time?

Scanning large photo libraries (50,000+ photos) may take several minutes because all processing happens on your device. You can pause and resume the scan at any time. The app is designed to handle libraries with 100,000+ photos without crashing.

Does PhotoSlim work on iPad?

Yes. PhotoSlim is fully optimized for both iPhone and iPad with a native layout that takes advantage of the larger screen for side-by-side photo comparison.
